## Strategic Tips for Engaging Alongside Capital Ships

Operating in close formation with large naval warships requires specific piloting habits. Whether flying close air support in a medium fighter or piloting a torpedo bomber, understanding capital firing patterns keeps friendly pilots alive.

### 1. Maintain Clear Turret Trajectories
The broadside turrets of a destroyer create massive kill zones around the vessel. Friendly strike pilots should never cross firing lanes directly in front of active capital turrets. Weapon convergence calculations mean automated batteries will prioritize hostile hulls, and stray crossfire can instantly vaporize a light escort fighter.

### 2. Prioritize Torpedo Screening
Capital destroyers excel at dueling other capital ships, but high-yield torpedo salvos launched by hostile strike bombers pose an acute threat. Escorting dogfighters should focus on intercepting inbound munitions rather than chasing fast skirmishers away from the fleet's defensive umbrella.

### 3. Coordinate Shield Disruption
Synchronize your missile volleys with the heavy broadsides of the capital warship. Striking an enemy hull immediately after the destroyer's main cannons collapse a shield quadrant ensures maximum kinetic damage to internal power plants and thruster arrays.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can players pilot the Squadron 42 Javelin directly in the single-player campaign?
In the cinematic campaign, narrative pacing focuses primarily on the player’s journey as a combat pilot advancing through frontline fighter squadrons. While players interact heavily with the warship, explore its multi-deck interior, and fight alongside it during fleet setpieces, general capital navigation remains under the command of naval bridge officers.

### How does the Javelin differ from the Idris frigate in combat?
The Javelin is nearly double the size of an Idris frigate and trades internal fighter capacity for raw direct-fire naval bombardment. While the Idris serves primarily as an operational patrol frigate with a spinal weapon and small flight wing, the destroyer carries heavier armor plating, dual capital turrets, and extensive broadside batteries designed to slug it out directly with alien capital combatants.

### Are the interiors of capital ships fully rendered during missions?
Yes. Design demonstrations and player reports confirm that capital vessels feature fully modeled, seamless interiors without artificial loading screens. Hallways, crew quarters, bridge stations, engineering reactors, and escape pod bays can be explored on foot, reinforcing environmental immersion during story cinematics and boarding actions.

### What role does the Squadron 42 Javelin play in anti-Vanduul defense?
Against heavy Vanduul incursions, the warship serves as an anchor for planetary defense and staging operations. Its defensive flak batteries suppress swarming hostile fighters, while its main forward rail and energy arrays crack open heavily armored Vanduul capital ships, protecting vital human settlements across the contested frontier.
