## Practical Tips to Complete the Campaign Efficiently

If you want to experience the story without getting bogged down in endless checkpoint reloads, adopting deliberate tactical habits makes a noticeable difference. Use these proven gameplay tips to streamline your completion time:

* **Remap and Master Flight Decoupling:** Decoupling your ship’s thrusters lets you maintain forward momentum while pivoting your nose to fire at pursuers. Mastering this skill cuts dogfight durations in half.
* **Prioritize Power Management:** Divert power to forward shields during head-on passes and route power to engines when retreating to break target locks.
* **Scan Targets Before Engaging:** Identifying enemy weapon loadouts prevents costly mistakes, such as jousting against high-caliber ballistic cannons.
* **Utilize Wingman Commands Early:** Do not attempt to eliminate an entire strike group alone. Ordering your AI squadmates to suppress secondary targets keeps your cockpit clear.
* **Focus on Subsystem Targeting:** In larger engagements against gunboats or corvettes, disabling turrets or main thrusters neutralizes threats far quicker than chipping away at raw hull integrity.
