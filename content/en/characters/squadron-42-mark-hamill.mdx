## Advanced Performance Capture and Cinematic Immersion

Capturing an actor of Hamill's caliber required an unprecedented technological setup. Filmed predominantly at The Imaginarium Studios in the United Kingdom, the production used full-body optical motion capture rigs paired with high-definition head-mounted camera systems. This dual tracking recorded Hamill’s physical mannerisms, vocal nuances, and minute facial muscle movements simultaneously.

The engineering team then integrated these performances into the game's proprietary character rig. Unlike traditional games where cutscenes switch to high-detail offline renders, Squadron 42 maintains the exact same model fidelity when transitioning from a story sequence to player-controlled cockpit flight.
